# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want The Show Verse to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and languages (Spanish and English are both welcome here).
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, and the community features of the app when they are discussed here) and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@Psantoss26](https://github.com/Psantoss26), through a [private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) (it is private, so it works for conduct reports too) or by contacting them through their GitHub profile. Every report will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. Depending on the case, the response goes from a private warning to a permanent ban.
